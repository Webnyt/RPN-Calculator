RPN Calculator

This calculator has no equals key. You put numbers on a stack, and each operator takes the top two off, works them out, and puts the answer back. To add 3 and 4, press 3, Enter, 4, then +.

The stack

0 numbers

    X is the top. Operators always use X and Y, then push the answer back as the new X.

      Enter pushes

      Enter puts the number you typed on top of the stack. Press Enter on an empty entry and it copies the top number, which is handy for squaring.

      Operators pop two, push one

      Plus pops X and Y, adds them, and pushes the sum. The two numbers it needs are always the two pushed most recently, which is exactly what a stack hands over first.

      Undo is a stack too

      Every change saves the stack before it happens, on a second stack. Undo pops the latest saved copy and puts it back, so the most recent step is always undone first.
